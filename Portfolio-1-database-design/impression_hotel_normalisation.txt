# Impression Hotel — Database Normalisation

## 1. First Normal Form (1NF)

A table is in First Normal Form when each field contains a single, atomic value and there are no repeating groups of columns.

In the Impression Hotel database, each guest has a separate record in the GUESTS table. Each booking has its own booking_id, guest_id, room_id, check_in, check_out, and booking_status fields. Each payment is recorded as an individual record in the PAYMENTS table.

Therefore, the tables follow the principles of 1NF because their fields store individual values rather than lists of values in a single field.

## 2. Second Normal Form (2NF)

A table is in Second Normal Form when it is in 1NF and every non-key attribute depends on the whole primary key.

The Impression Hotel tables use single-column primary keys, such as guest_id, room_id, booking_id, and payment_id. Since these primary keys are not composite keys, there are no partial dependencies on part of a composite key.

The design therefore satisfies the main requirements of 2NF, assuming the stated functional dependencies hold.

## 3. Third Normal Form (3NF)

A table is in Third Normal Form when it is in 2NF and non-key attributes do not depend transitively on a primary key through another non-key attribute.

The database separates guests, room types, rooms, bookings, and payments into different tables.

For example, the nightly rate and type name are stored in ROOM_TYPES rather than being repeated for every room or booking. The ROOMS table references ROOM_TYPES through room_type_id. Guest details are stored in GUESTS rather than repeated in every booking.

This separation helps reduce unnecessary duplication and update anomalies.

## Conclusion

The Impression Hotel database uses separate related tables and primary and foreign keys to organise information. Its design follows the principles of 1NF and 2NF and is structured to support 3NF, provided the intended functional dependencies are maintained. Constraints such as UNIQUE, NOT NULL, CHECK, and FOREIGN KEY further help preserve data quality and consistency.
